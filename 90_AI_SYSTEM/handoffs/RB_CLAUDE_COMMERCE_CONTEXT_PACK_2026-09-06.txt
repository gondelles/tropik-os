2. CURRENT RE:BUILD BUSINESS / CLAIM STATE
==================================================

Re:Build does not perform demolition.

Working scope:
authorized debris source -> routing/loading coordination -> transport -> controlled receiving site -> safety inspection -> sorting -> recovery -> processing -> testing -> released product -> sale/redeployment/trial -> authorized residual pathway.

CURRENT STRATEGIC POSITION:

- validate one 90-day single-hub pilot before network scale;
- Santa Eduvigis / Urimare is the preferred first validation target, using only a provisional 12,000 m2 planning cell;
- Santa is NOT an authorized Re:Build operating site yet;
- Catia La Mar remains fallback/large-hub alternative;
- Caraballeda / Los Corales remains source-side intake/sort/transfer option;
- commissioning target remains 25 -> 50 -> 75 t/day, subject to HSE/feedstock/QA/offtake gates;
- no heavy-equipment purchase before pilot evidence;
- material sales alone are NOT presented as self-funding;
- public Needs Registry and measured Impact Dashboard are desired future public layers;
- public financial donation/payment collection remains gated by legal/accounting/payment/founder approval;
- the website MAY collect structured non-binding offers, capabilities, buyer interest/specifications, workforce interest, authority referrals and funding-partner inquiries;
- a submission is interest/offer received, NOT verified evidence or a commitment.

PROHIBITED PUBLIC CLAIMS UNTIL VERIFIED:

- Santa is allocated/authorized to Re:Build;
- assumed Santa area is a confirmed operating polygon;
- Re:Build currently processes a stated tonnage;
- a stated diversion/recovery percentage is achieved;
- products are generally COVENIN-approved merely because benchmark standards are referenced;
- jobs have already been generated without payroll/work evidence;
- CO2/carbon/environmental benefit percentages;
- donors/funders/equipment support are secured when only discussed;
- pledged resources are received/deployed;
- material sales alone finance the pilot;
- donations are tax-deductible or charitable until approved.
