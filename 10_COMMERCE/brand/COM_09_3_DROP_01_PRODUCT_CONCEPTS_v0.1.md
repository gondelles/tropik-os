## 2. Final Founder-Approved Concepts

### C1 — P07 `Pura bulla lo tuyo`

- **Product:** T-shirt.
- **Lane:** A — Statement, primary path.
- **Placement:** front center-chest; bold large-scale typography; one dominant gesture.
- **Garment/base-color intent:** Ink or Ivory base; selective Sun or Leaf accent. Intent only; no supplier/material commitment.
- **Market:** BOTH — `USA-PORTABLE / CULTURALLY ROOTED`.
- **Rationale:** strongest portable shortlisted phrase (36/40); protects the USA track while remaining culturally rooted.

#### Safe default visual path
Pure typography / original graphic treatment with no identifiable real-person imagery.

#### Alternate path — gated
Lane D / photo-led execution may be explored only if Tropik obtains appropriate rights/clearance for the specific source and identifiable subject. Founder confirmation that a reference subject is an adult does not itself establish commercial likeness/publicity rights.

#### Validation
- phrase remains strong without a photo mechanic;
- legible at ecommerce-thumbnail and moving-body scale;
- visual treatment remains Calle rather than generic meme merch.

### C2 — P13 `Si no te gusta la velocidad, oríllate`

- **Product:** T-shirt or hoodie.
- **Lane:** B — Back Story, primary path.
- **Placement:** minimal front hit + full phrase on back in a multi-line composition.
- **Garment/base-color intent:** Ink/dark base with Ivory and selective Sun contrast. Intent only.
- **Market:** BOTH — `CROSS-MARKET`.
- **Rationale:** only CROSS-MARKET line in the initial shortlist; Lane B gives the seven-word phrase enough space and creates a strong back-graphic opportunity.

#### Safe default visual path
Original abstract/generic motorsport-energy treatment: for example, a non-identifiable overturned race-car silhouette or abstract speed/crash geometry, with no real driver likeness, no recognizable team livery, no sponsor/team/company marks and no copied race photograph.

#### Alternate path — gated
Founder proposed a real-world motorsport crash moment as inspiration. Do not use or reproduce any recognizable named driver, team, sponsor, livery, event photograph or signature moment commercially unless rights/trademark/publicity issues are separately cleared. Transforming/redrawing a source does not automatically clear those issues.

#### Validation
- back-graphic typography remains legible;
- concept still works if the visual is fully generic/original;
- no third-party motorsport equity becomes the core reason the product works.
