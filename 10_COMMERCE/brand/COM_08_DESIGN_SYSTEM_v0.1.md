## 2. Working Design Tokens

### Color
- Ink `#171717`
- Ivory `#F6F2E8`
- Sun `#F6B218`
- Leaf `#0F5A46`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Mist `#ECEBE7`

Semantic mapping — POC:
- text-primary → Ink
- surface-main → Ivory
- accent-primary → Sun
- accent-secondary → Leaf
- surface-card → White / Mist only
- surface-dark → Ink

### Color usage rule — FOUNDER FEEDBACK
- Sun is an accent, CTA, badge or active-state color; not a dominant card/background system.
- Category cards should be visually consistent as one family. Do not assign arbitrary White / Ink / Sun backgrounds to adjacent category cards.
- Leaf is a secondary brand accent / editorial field, not a default full-screen hero color.
- Large areas should primarily use Ivory, White, Mist, photography/editorial imagery, and selective Ink fields.

### Typography — FOUNDER PREFERRED POC DIRECTION
**Direction C — Expressive Optimist** from Tropik Typography Exploration v0.1:
- Display: Bricolage Grotesque 700/800
- Body/UI: Lexend 300/400/500
- Yellow/Sun details used selectively as accent, never as low-contrast body text.

Status: APPROVED FOR POC / NOT YET FINAL CANONICAL TYPOGRAPHY.

Usage:
- Bricolage: hero/H1, large H2/editorial statements only.
- Lexend: navigation, product titles, prices, body, controls, labels and buttons.
- Prices use tabular numerals where supported.
- Mobile readability has priority over decorative display treatment.

Implementation for POC:
- external web-font loading may be used temporarily on staging for rapid evaluation;
- production should prefer WordPress Font Library or self-hosted optimized WOFF2 after approval;
- `font-display: swap` and system fallbacks required.

### Radius — POC
- category/product image: 24px
- editorial image/container: 24–32px
- small card: 18–24px
- button/pill: 999px

### Spacing
Use a consistent XS / S / M / L / XL / 2XL scale.
Prefer strong negative space and fewer, larger compositional zones over many small boxed sections.

## 3. Home POC Visual Direction — REVIEWED 2026-08-23

### KEEP
- centered prominent Tropik logo;
- thin minimal header;
- image-led/editorial hero rather than a flat color-only hero;
- hero copy separated clearly from the image on mobile;
- strong Bricolage display typography;
- Sun pill CTA and small Sun badges/details;
- consistent rounded category cards with imagery;
- product grid with large image area and restrained product metadata;
- split editorial / Our Story section;
- compact reassurance strip;
- dark Ink footer;
- significant Ivory/White negative space.

### REFINE
- Header icons must share one horizontal baseline and equal hit-area sizing.
- Desktop header target remains: `[☰ SHOP] — [centered TROPIK logo] — [Search] [Account] [Cart]`.
- Mobile: `[☰] — [centered TROPIK] — [Cart]`; Search/Account may move into drawer.
- Category cards must use the same surface/image treatment; differentiation comes from imagery/copy, not arbitrary card colors.
- Category card text should remain legible over imagery via controlled gradient/overlay if needed.
- Product cards should avoid unnecessary borders; image quality and whitespace carry the hierarchy.
- Editorial sections should alternate rhythm without becoming a stack of identical boxes.
- Footer email signup remains YELLOW until email platform/process is validated.

### REMOVE / DO NOT CARRY FORWARD
- Current v0.2 flat full-Leaf hero as final direction.
- Adjacent White / Ink / Sun category-card backgrounds.
- Misaligned/staggered header icons.
- Placeholder business/policy claims generated by AI unless separately validated.

## 4. AI-Mockup Truth Guardrail
Claude Design / image generation may invent realistic ecommerce details. These are **not FACTS or DECISIONS**.

The following examples from design explorations are NOT approved and must not be published until verified:
- specific product prices;
- `8 products` or any SKU count;
- `DROP 01 — LIMITED` / scarcity language;
- `30-day returns`;
- `Mid-weight cotton`;
- `Region set at checkout`;
- `Card and local methods`;
- `© Tropik Goods LLC` or any legal-entity wording;
- `free carbon-neutral delivery` or environmental/logistics claims;
- material/fit/shipping details not sourced from actual product/supplier data.

Mocks should use neutral placeholders where truth is not yet established.

## 6. Asset Generation Guidelines
Every generated asset brief should specify:
- use case;
- aspect ratio;
- subject;
- environment;
- lighting;
- visual mood;
- negative-space requirement;
- required/prohibited product details;
- text embedded? default NO;
- variants requested.

Do not promote this Design System to PRIVATE_CANONICAL until:
- founder reviews brand foundations;
- palette contrast/accessibility is tested;
- POC demonstrates tokens on real layouts;
- typography is tested in real WooCommerce UI;
- mobile behavior is reviewed;
