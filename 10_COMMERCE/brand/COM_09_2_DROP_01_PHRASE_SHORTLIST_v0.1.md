## 1. Final Scoring Rubric — Founder Approved

Each criterion is scored 1–5, maximum 40:

1. **Ownability** — distinct enough to merit development.
2. **Emotional Resonance** — recognition, humor, memory, warmth or attitude.
3. **Wearability** — realistic repeat-wear potential.
4. **Visual Potential** — potential for strong typography/placement/graphic treatment.
5. **Portability** — how well the concept functions within its declared market tag, not how universal it is.
6. **Brand Fit** — feels like Tropik Calle rather than generic meme merch.
7. **Risk / Clearance Safety** — 5 = low risk; 1 = high risk.
8. **Specificity** — distinctly rooted in Calle rather than swappable generic “Latin lifestyle”.

### Thresholds
- **33–40 — SHORTLIST**
- **29–32 — ITERATE / HOLD**
- **28 or less — REJECT**

### Hard rules
- Wearability ≤2 → REJECT.
- Brand Fit ≤2 → REJECT.
- Specificity ≤2 → REJECT.
- Risk ≤2 → REJECT or ESCALATE.
- Clear dependency on third-party phrase/logo/likeness/artwork/equity → REJECT or ESCALATE.
- If a phrase is presented as colloquial Venezuelan speech and authenticity is uncertain → `HOLD FOR FOUNDER/NATIVE REVIEW`, regardless of total.
- `AUTHENTICITY CHECK — FOUNDER` and `HOLD FOR FOUNDER/NATIVE REVIEW` are the same gate.
- Do not lower the 33-point SHORTLIST threshold automatically to fill quota. Strong 29–32 concepts may be explicitly carried into iteration and rescored.

### Portability scoring clarification
A `REGION-LIMITED` concept can receive 5/5 Portability if it functions strongly and honestly within its intended regional/cultural audience. Portability measures fit to the declared tag, not universal English-language comprehension.

Valid tags:
- `CROSS-MARKET`
- `USA-PORTABLE / CULTURALLY ROOTED`
- `REGION-LIMITED`
