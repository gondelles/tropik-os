# COM_01_VENTURE_BRIEF

Status: PRIVATE_CANONICAL  
Version: 1.0

## Venture

**Tropik Commerce** operates the consumer-facing **Tropik** brand under Tropik Goods.

## Current business model

### LATAM
- limited-stock apparel/merch;
- protect cash by keeping initial inventory constrained;
- validate products, demand, pricing and operations before larger buys.

### USA
- print-on-demand as the preferred low-inventory starting model;
- WooCommerce is the owned store;
- tools such as Printify may be evaluated when needed;
- Etsy may be considered later if it supports validated products and does not create unnecessary operational complexity.

### Amazon
Future distribution path. It is not an immediate execution priority and should not drive inventory commitments before product/operational validation.

## Existing asset

An existing WooCommerce site is hosted and available for relaunch. Its technical/business state has not yet been canonically audited.

Existing domain: `tropikgoods.com`.

## Phase objective

Prepare the smallest reliable commerce system capable of supporting validated pilot products and a controlled relaunch.

## Initial execution sequence

1. audit current WooCommerce state;
2. convert findings into prioritized backlog;
3. distinguish LATAM vs USA operating requirements;
4. define pilot products and unit economics;
5. validate product pages, cart, checkout, payment/shipping behavior and transaction communications;
6. establish minimum analytics/measurement;
7. run controlled relaunch/validation.

## Principles

- reuse working assets before replacing them;
- no unnecessary paid plugins or redesign projects;
- validation before inventory/ad scale;
- avoid a large catalog initially;
- preserve evidence and decisions in Tropik OS;
- sanitize anything later promoted to a public portfolio artifact.
