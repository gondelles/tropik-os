## Strategic principles

1. Validate before scaling.
2. Protect cash and focus.
3. Prefer reversible decisions under uncertainty.
4. Prefer simple adequate tools.
5. Manual process before complex automation.
6. Separate LATAM stock and USA POD when economics/logistics differ.
7. Preserve reusable learning/IP.
8. AI assists and challenges; it does not become unreviewed authority.
9. Human approval for sensitive, costly, public, legal/fiscal or irreversible actions.
