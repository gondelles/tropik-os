## Boundaries

**HQ:** portfolio governance, prioritization, capital discipline, AI OS, cross-venture learning.

**Commerce:** store, products, unit economics, checkout, logistics, fulfillment, customer experience, Amazon readiness.

**Habit App:** user/problem, PRD, prototype, code, product analytics.

**Education:** knowledge mining, market opportunity, curriculum, production, Udemy listing, student feedback.

**Shared:** only assets genuinely reusable across ventures.

Do not force shared systems where venture economics or workflows materially differ.
