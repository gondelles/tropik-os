## Purpose

Tropik HQ contains cross-venture operating context for Tropik Goods. Detailed venture data belongs in its venture folder/project.

## Canonical brand hierarchy

- Tropik Goods = umbrella / venture system.
- Tropik = commerce / merch brand.
- Habit App = software venture, working name.
- Tropik Academy = internal education label; public brand not locked.

Do not reverse Tropik Goods and Tropik.

## Authority hierarchy

When information conflicts, use this order:

1. Latest explicit human approval/correction.
2. Approved Strategic Decision Log.
3. Current State and Priorities.
4. Master Brief.
5. Venture-specific canonical context.
6. Primary source data.
7. Verified external research.
8. AI analysis.
9. AI idea/hypothesis.

A future goal is not automatically an active priority.
