# RB_MASTER_BUSINESS_PLAN_BLUEPRINT_v0.1

**Status:** PRIVATE_WORKING  
**Venture:** Tropik Re:Build  
**Updated:** 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Canonical structure for the complete Re:Build business plan. The structure is adapted from the founder's previous thesis/business-plan method: problem -> market study -> technical study -> organization/legal -> economic/financial study -> scenarios -> conclusions, expanded for disaster recovery, grants, impact funding and material recovery.

## Core scope

Re:Build does not perform demolition.

Core operating chain:

`authorized debris source -> collection/loading coordination -> transport -> receiving -> safety screening -> sorting -> recovery -> processing -> testing -> products -> reconstruction/offtake`

### 2. Objectives
#### General objective
Design, validate and deploy a safe, economically credible circular debris-recovery system in La Guaira that converts approved post-disaster material into useful reconstruction inputs while creating paid local work.

#### Specific objectives
- validate feedstock and routing;
- select/authorize MVP site;
- define technical process;
- validate recovered products;
- define community workforce model;
- obtain non-dilutive/in-kind funding where possible;
- prove pilot economics;
- define commercial scale path.

### 5. Business Model
Three linked engines:
1. Recovery / service and subsidized public-good layer.
2. Recovered-material product revenue.
3. Livelihood/employment and social-impact layer.

Define who funds each activity, who owns recovered material, and how the system transitions from subsidy-supported pilot to increasing earned revenue.

### 30. Risk Register
Fatal assumptions include:
- no authorized site;
- no authority to receive/reroute debris;
- recovered material cannot legally be sold/redeployed;
- hazardous contamination overwhelms sorting;
- products fail specification;
- no buyer/reconstruction use;
- transport/equipment costs destroy economics;
- funding restrictions incompatible with private commercialization;
- operational safety cannot be controlled.
