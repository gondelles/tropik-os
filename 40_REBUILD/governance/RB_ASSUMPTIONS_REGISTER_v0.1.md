## Purpose

Track every important Re:Build assumption that is not yet verified locally. Assumptions must not silently become public claims or capital commitments.

## Classification

- **VERIFIED FACT** — supported by credible evidence.
- **STRATEGIC DECISION** — current operating choice.
- **WORKING HYPOTHESIS** — plausible assumption requiring validation.
- **TARGET** — internal performance objective.
- **ASPIRATION** — future ambition.
- **PROHIBITED CLAIM** — must not be stated publicly as fact until validated.

- Re:Build products are structural-grade.
- Re:Build products reduce carbon by a stated percentage.
- Re:Build will pay every worker USD 600/month from commercial revenue.
- Re:Build owns or controls any public debris site.
- Re:Build has government, UNDP, CAF or NGO funding unless formally awarded.
