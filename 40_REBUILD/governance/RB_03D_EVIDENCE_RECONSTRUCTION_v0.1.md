# 5. Five most decision-changing evidence packages now missing

## 1 — Santa authority + output-rights package

**Why #1:** without legal/operational authority and explicit output rights, neither processing nor monetization/redeployment can proceed regardless of economics.

Required minimum:
- valid signer and authority basis;
- defined Re:Build scope/time;
- debris-routing permission/path;
- output ownership/redeployment/sale rules;
- service/gate-fee payer authority if applicable.

## 2 — Santa physical/HSE/environment + suspect/residual package

**Why #2:** a nominal 12,000 m² cell is irrelevant if the usable polygon, drainage, receptors, truck movements or waste-exception pathways fail.

Required minimum:
- GPS/surveyed operating polygon and exclusions;
- truck turning/access evidence;
- drainage/flood/receptor screen;
- accountable HSE lead / host rules;
- authorized suspect/hazardous pathway;
- authorized residual route and fee.

## 3 — Routing + measured feedstock characterization

**Why #3:** throughput, recovery yield, equipment duty, residual burden and product candidate all depend on what actually arrives.

Required minimum:
- recent load/dispatch records;
- authorized source/routing schedule;
- source-stratified weighed dataset, target 30 loads / ~600 t when practical;
- contamination + mass-balance QA.

## 4 — First buyer specification + technical trial path + lab route

**Why #4:** no first mineral product should be selected or produced without a real bounded use.

Required minimum:
- exact buyer/project specification;
- named technical approver;
- recycled-material trial pathway;
- required lab methods/limits;
- trial quantity/site/date and field PASS/FAIL criteria;
- written disposition path;
- local lab quote/competence/turnaround.

## 5 — Quote-backed resource + workforce + payer stack

**Why #5:** this converts the ROM into the actual 90-day cash/resource requirement and decides whether the capital-light thesis survives.

Required minimum:
- host-provided equipment/scale/water/security/utilities list;
- loader/excavator terms;
- crusher/screen campaign/shared terms;
- inbound/outbound truck rates and payer;
- workforce rates/inclusions/legal treatment;
- documented payer/in-kind/funding route.
