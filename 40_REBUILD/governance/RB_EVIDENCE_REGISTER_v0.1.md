## New RB-03B evidence

### RB-E-019 — COVENIN 2000:1987 provides relevant Venezuelan base/subbase test requirements
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT — STANDARD TEXT / DESK RESEARCH  
The road specification states for natural/mixed granzón base benchmarks LL ≤25%, PI ≤6%, CBR ≥80% heavy traffic / ≥60% light traffic, and 95% modified-Proctor maximum dry density for compacted natural granzón. For crushed-stone base it specifies clean/resistant material, COVENIN 266 wear ≤50%, COVENIN 1124 fractured faces, LL ≤25%, PI ≤6%, Type 1/2/3 gradation envelopes and CBR ≥80%, with prior written Ministry approval.  
Source copy used: https://mauricioravanal.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/2000-1-1987-esp-y-cod-carreteras.pdf  
Implication: strong Venezuelan benchmark for Road Base trials, **not automatic approval of recycled C&D aggregate**. LOCAL ENGINEERING SPECIFICATION TBD.

### RB-E-026 — Venezuelan waste law treats debris/scrap as special-handling categories and creates authorization obligations
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PRELIMINARY LEGAL DESK RESEARCH  
The Ley de Gestión Integral de la Basura includes escombros and chatarras among special-handling wastes and regulates handling/authorization; legal interpretation for the proposed site/output remains specialist work.  
Accessible copy/reference: https://studylib.es/doc/9033732/ley-de-gestion-integral-de-la-basura  
Implication: output ownership, site/handler authorization and fill/redeployment legality are fatal legal gates. Venezuelan counsel required.

### RB-E-027 — COVENIN 2253:2001 contains crystalline-silica occupational exposure limits
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / HSE STANDARD REFERENCE
