# RB_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_v0.1

**Status:** PRIVATE_WORKING  
**Venture:** Tropik Re:Build  
**Updated:** 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Record evidence that materially changes Re:Build strategy. Public evidence does not substitute for field validation, but it may justify where to focus validation.

## Evidence

### RB-E-001 — Debris scale
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
UNDP reported on 24 July 2026 that approximately **2.1 million tonnes** of earthquake debris/waste remained across the hardest-hit areas and equated that to approximately **105,000 20-tonne truckloads**.

Source: https://www.undp.org/latin-america/press-releases/venezuela-earthquakes-debris-estimate-nearly-doubles-21-million-tonnes-undp-says

Implication: Re:Build should treat a 90-day MVP as a proof-of-system pilot, not as a meaningful percentage of the regional cleanup.

### RB-E-002 — Comprehensive debris management is already a policy direction
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
UNDP states that managing the debris requires risk assessment, material sorting, recovery of reusable resources, identification of hazardous waste, and safe/environmentally responsible disposal. UNDP's Venezuela recovery page also describes support for sorting, recycling and reuse.

Sources:
- https://www.undp.org/latin-america/press-releases/venezuela-earthquakes-debris-estimate-nearly-doubles-21-million-tonnes-undp-says
- https://www.undp.org/latin-america/venezuela

Implication: Re:Build should integrate with the recovery architecture rather than pitch recycling as a parallel concept disconnected from official operations.

### RB-E-003 — Dedicated debris-management funding exists
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
The Republic of Korea approved a **USD 3 million** contribution through UNDP for debris clearance and waste-management activities following the earthquakes.

Source: https://www.undp.org/es/venezuela/noticias/republic-korea-approves-usd-3-million-support-debris-clearance-and-emergency-relief-communities-affected-earthquakes-venezuela

Implication: non-dilutive/institutional financing is a realistic category to pursue, though Re:Build has no award or entitlement to it.

### RB-E-004 — CAF recovery fund exists
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
CAF established the Venezuela Recovery and Reconstruction Fund with **USD 1 million seed capital**, designed to mobilize public, private and international resources. Eligible recovery areas include logistics, debris removal, reconstruction and livelihoods/income generation.

Sources:
- https://www.caf.com/en/currently/news/caf-establishes-venezuela-recovery-and-reconstruction-fund/
- https://www.caf.com/en/specials/recovery-and-reconstruction-fund-for-venezuela/

Implication: a Re:Build pilot concept should be written in a form that can be evaluated as recovery infrastructure + livelihoods + material reuse, not merely as a startup funding request.

### RB-E-005 — Existing temporary debris centers reduce the need for a greenfield site
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Public reporting in July 2026 described **11 temporary disposal/classification centers** in La Guaira, including centers in Caraballeda and Catia La Mar. Publicly identified locations include Macuto/Las Quince Letras, Punta Tanaguarena, Catia La Mar/Avenida El Ejército, Avenida Balneario, Atanasio Girardot, Caribe Campo/Carmen de Uria, and Los Corales.

Sources:
- https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1559088-11-centros-procesan-escombros/
- https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1563520-escombros-terremoto-la-guaira/

Implication: preferred MVP architecture is a dedicated Re:Build lane inside/adjacent to an authorized site, subject to confirmation.

### RB-E-006 — Santa Eduvigis is a major final-processing/disposal node
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Public reporting identifies Parque Tecnológico Ambiental / relleno sanitario **Santa Eduvigis**, in the Urimare area, as a final authorized debris destination. Reports describe a roughly **48-hectare** facility and two prepared terraces of approximately **5 ha and 6 ha** for safe debris placement/processing.

Sources:
- https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1560893-escombros-relleno-santa-eduvigis-no-costas/
- https://mazo4f.com/vicepresidente-sectorial-de-obras-publicas-verifico-esquema-de-procesamiento-de-escombros-en-la-guaira

Implication: Santa Eduvigis is Candidate Site #1 for field validation because the receiving authority, environmental controls and heavy-equipment ecosystem may already exist.

### RB-E-007 — Existing processing system already includes separation and crushing
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Public reporting describes mechanical processing at temporary centers: separation of steel/metal from concrete/mineral material, crushing, and reuse of crushed materials for land-based infrastructure applications. One public source states that steel, concrete and plastics are separated and that crushing can reduce material volume substantially.

Sources:
- https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1563520-escombros-terremoto-la-guaira/
- https://www.ciudadccs.info/publicacion/47013

Implication: Re:Build should not duplicate equipment that can be accessed through an existing authorized processing train. The MVP should first test a partnership/shared-equipment model.

### RB-E-008 — Caraballeda / Los Corales remains a major active debris zone
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
A late-August 2026 public update reported more than **200,000 m³** removed in the El Palmar / Los Corales area of Caraballeda and more than **40 machines** active there, with debris being moved to a collection center in the same area.

Source: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1585710-ministerio-de-obras-publicas-6/

Implication: Caraballeda is Candidate Site #2 as a source-adjacent eastern satellite/pilot lane.

### RB-E-009 — Authorized coastal disposal is an environmental priority
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Authorities deployed environmental monitoring to prevent inappropriate coastal disposal and publicly identified Santa Eduvigis and Caraballeda authorized destinations.

Source: https://www.ciudadccs.info/publicacion/46981

Implication: coastal-risk rubble may be a high-priority recovery stream, but only when routed through authorized channels.

### RB-E-010 — An expert publicly estimated ~30% reuse potential
**Classification:** WORKING EXTERNAL REFERENCE, NOT RE:BUILD FACT  
Structural/disaster-recovery engineer Kit Miyamoto was publicly reported as estimating that roughly **30%** of debris may be reusable, with steel/metals and concrete among potential reuse streams.

Source: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1559877-ingeniero-japones-kit-miyamoto-la-guaira/

Implication: use only as a hypothesis range for early planning. Re:Build must measure its own recovery yield before making any claim.

### RB-E-011 — Private recycling capacity is participating
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
La Guaira Corporación de Reciclaje publicly described private-sector participation in processing recovered materials and reinserting them into national industry.

Source: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1582018-empresa-privada-escombros/

Implication: Re:Build should map existing recyclers as partners/buyers rather than assume it must process every stream itself.

### RB-E-012 — Disaster C&D staging requires segregation and environmental controls
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / REFERENCE GUIDANCE  
US EPA disaster-debris guidance states that recyclable and non-recyclable C&D debris should be segregated, hazardous materials separated, staging areas secured, water/flood risks considered, and releases to the environment prevented. EPA references separation distances from surface water and residential/public structures as good-practice siting guidance.

Sources:
- https://iwaste.epa.gov/guidance/natural-disaster/fact-sheets/types-of-waste?id=construction-demolition
- https://www.epa.gov/large-scale-residential-demolition/managing-stormwater-and-dust-demolition-sites

Implication: Re:Build MVP design requires hard environmental controls even when operating in a temporary/authorized recovery site.
