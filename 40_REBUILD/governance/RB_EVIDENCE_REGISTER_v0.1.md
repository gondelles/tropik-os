# RB_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_v0.1

**Status:** PRIVATE_WORKING  
**Venture:** Tropik Re:Build  
**Updated:** 2026-09-06 — RB-03A

## Purpose
Record evidence that materially changes Re:Build strategy. Public evidence does not substitute for field validation, but it may justify where to focus validation.

## Evidence

### RB-E-001 — Debris scale
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
UNDP reported 24 July 2026 that approximately **2.1 million tonnes** of debris/waste remained across the hardest-hit areas, equivalent to ~105,000 20-t trucks. This denominator is not a precise current La-Guaira-only feedstock figure.  
Source: https://www.undp.org/latin-america/press-releases/venezuela-earthquakes-debris-estimate-nearly-doubles-21-million-tonnes-undp-says  
Implication: MVP optimizes evidence, not regional cleanup share.

### RB-E-002 — Comprehensive debris management is policy-aligned
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
UNDP describes debris management as risk assessment, sorting, reusable-resource recovery, hazardous-waste identification and safe/environmentally responsible disposal; its Venezuela recovery page describes sorting, recycling and reuse.  
Sources: UNDP debris release above; https://www.undp.org/latin-america/venezuela  
Implication: integrate with authorized recovery architecture.

### RB-E-003 — Dedicated debris-management funding exists
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
Republic of Korea approved **USD 3 million** through UNDP for debris clearance/waste management and related early recovery.  
Source: https://www.undp.org/es/venezuela/noticias/republic-korea-approves-usd-3-million-support-debris-clearance-and-emergency-relief-communities-affected-earthquakes-venezuela  
Implication: institutional/non-dilutive funding is a real category; Re:Build has no entitlement.

### RB-E-004 — CAF Recovery and Reconstruction Fund exists
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT  
CAF launched a Venezuela Recovery and Reconstruction Fund with **USD 1 million seed capital** and action areas including debris removal, infrastructure, livelihoods, logistics, data/monitoring and reconstruction.  
Sources: https://www.caf.com/en/currently/news/caf-establishes-venezuela-recovery-and-reconstruction-fund/ ; https://www.caf.com/en/specials/recovery-and-reconstruction-fund-for-venezuela/  
Implication: Re:Build should be fundable as recovery infrastructure + livelihoods + auditable material reuse if eligible.

### RB-E-005 — Temporary debris centers exist
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Public reporting described multiple temporary disposal/classification centers in La Guaira, including Caraballeda and Catia La Mar locations.  
Sources: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1559088-11-centros-procesan-escombros/ ; https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1563520-escombros-terremoto-la-guaira/  
Implication: validate a dedicated lane inside/adjacent to an authorized site before greenfield land.

### RB-E-006 — Santa Eduvigis is a major final node
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Public reporting identifies Santa Eduvigis / Urimare as an authorized final debris destination and describes ~48 ha total facility area plus two prepared terraces of ~5 ha and ~6 ha.  
Sources: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1560893-escombros-relleno-santa-eduvigis-no-costas/ ; https://mazo4f.com/vicepresidente-sectorial-de-obras-publicas-verifico-esquema-de-procesamiento-de-escombros-en-la-guaira  
Implication: Candidate #1 for field-validation and partnership discussion.

### RB-E-007 — Existing system already separates/crushes some debris
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Public reporting describes separation of steel, concrete and plastics and mechanical crushing in temporary centers.  
Sources: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1563520-escombros-terremoto-la-guaira/ ; https://www.ciudadccs.info/publicacion/47013  
Implication: shared-equipment/host-train access can be superior to duplicating CAPEX.

### RB-E-008 — Caraballeda / Los Corales active debris zone
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Late-August public reporting described >200,000 m³ removed around El Palmar/Los Corales and >40 machines active.  
Source: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1585710-ministerio-de-obras-publicas-6/  
Implication: Candidate #2 source-adjacent satellite/pilot lane.

### RB-E-009 — Coastal disposal is under environmental scrutiny
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
Authorities deployed environmental monitoring to prevent inappropriate coastal disposal and identified authorized destinations including Santa Eduvigis and Caraballeda.  
Source: https://www.ciudadccs.info/publicacion/46981  
Implication: no uncontrolled runoff/disposal; coastal-protection controls are core design requirements.

### RB-E-010 — External ~30% reuse estimate
**Classification:** WORKING EXTERNAL REFERENCE, NOT RE:BUILD FACT  
A public report attributed to engineer Kit Miyamoto an estimate that ~30% of debris may be reusable, including steel/metals and concrete.  
Source: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1559877-ingeniero-japones-kit-miyamoto-la-guaira/  
Implication: planning reference only; Re:Build must measure its own yields.

### RB-E-011 — Private recycling capacity is participating
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / PUBLIC REPORT  
La Guaira Corporación de Reciclaje publicly described private-sector participation in processing recovered materials and reinserting them into national industry.  
Source: https://www.laiguana.tv/articulos/1582018-empresa-privada-escombros/  
Implication: map recyclers as partners/offtakers rather than processing every stream internally.

### RB-E-012 — Disaster C&D staging needs segregation/environmental controls
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / REFERENCE GUIDANCE  
US EPA disaster-debris guidance emphasizes segregation, hazardous-material separation, secure staging, water/flood risk and prevention of environmental releases; EPA demolition guidance addresses stormwater and dust control.  
Sources: https://www.epa.gov/disaster-debris/planning-considerations-materials-and-wastes-disasters ; https://www.epa.gov/large-scale-residential-demolition/managing-stormwater-and-dust-demolition-sites  
Implication: authorized temporary status does not remove the need for HSE/environment controls.

### RB-E-013 — Reclaimed concrete can be technically suitable for granular base when processed/tested
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / REFERENCE GUIDANCE  
FHWA guidance documents use of processed reclaimed concrete material in granular base/subbase applications and identifies relevant properties including gradation, absorption, stability, strength, durability and drainage. It notes source separation/stockpile consistency and potential drainage/tufa concerns in some applications.  
Sources: https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/research/infrastructure/pavements/97148/053.cfm ; https://highways.fhwa.dot.gov/federal-lands/specs/wfl-los/fp-14-library  
Implication: Road Base is technically credible as a product family, but Venezuelan/local project specifications and batch testing remain mandatory.

### RB-E-014 — Crusher nameplate capacity can greatly exceed Re:Build MVP throughput
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / EQUIPMENT BENCHMARK  
Kleemann lists MC 100 EVO class feed capacity up to ~220 t/h and MC 110 EVO2 up to ~400 t/h.  
Source: https://www.wirtgen-group.com/ocs/en-ro/kleemann/mobile-jaw-crushers-80-c/  
Implication: for 25–150 t/day Re:Build should not size the business from crusher nameplate; sorting, feed preparation, QA, uptime and offtake are likely tighter constraints.

### RB-E-015 — Current global equipment market supports a wide crusher/screen purchase ROM
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / MARKET BENCHMARK, NOT VENEZUELA QUOTE  
2026 MachineryTrader listings show Kleemann MC100 mobile jaw crushers roughly in the ~$145k–$650k asking-price range and McCloskey R155 screens roughly ~$64k–$399k, with model/year/hour variation.  
Sources: https://www.machinerytrader.com/listings/for-sale/kleemann/mc100/crusher-aggregate-equipment/1003 ; https://www.machinerytrader.com/listings/for-sale/mccloskey/r155/screen-aggregate-equipment/1005  
Implication: purchasing core plant is capital intensive; rental/shared model is justified for MVP. Landed Venezuela costs remain unknown.

### RB-E-016 — WooCommerce mobile app supports core order/product management
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / SOFTWARE DOCUMENTATION  
WooCommerce documentation states its mobile app can view/manage orders and products, edit product details including inventory/stock status, and create/edit orders in current versions.  
Sources: https://woocommerce.com/document/woocommerce-ios/ ; https://woocommerce.com/document/products-ios/ ; https://woocommerce.com/document/creating-orders-on-the-mobile-app/  
Implication: mobile-first commercial administration is feasible without a custom app; safety/engineering master records should remain elsewhere.

### RB-E-017 — Free WordPress structured-content tooling exists
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / SOFTWARE REFERENCE  
WordPress.org lists Pods as a free framework capable of custom post types, taxonomies and fields; CPT UI also provides no-code CPT/taxonomy registration.  
Sources: https://wordpress.org/plugins/pods/ ; https://wordpress.org/plugins/custom-post-type-ui/  
Implication: Re:Build can structure process/evidence/project content without a paid custom platform; use the minimum plugin set.

### RB-E-018 — POD support-merch unit costs make inventory-free validation possible
**Classification:** VERIFIED FACT / CURRENT PLATFORM REFERENCE  
Printify currently lists common Bella+Canvas 3001 tee production from roughly low-double-digit USD/EUR equivalents depending provider/plan, and embroidered hats from roughly mid-teens upward; exact provider/shipping/tax costs vary.  
Sources: https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-pricing-calculator/ ; https://printify.com/custom-embroidered-hats/  
Implication: USA support merch can be validated POD-first; margins must be recalculated at launch.
