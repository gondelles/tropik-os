### Utilization implication
- 25 t/day: crusher ~8% of 8-h shift — shared/campaign hire strongly preferred.
- 50 t/day: ~16% — shared/campaign.
- 75 t/day: ~24% — campaign/weekly or guaranteed host access.
- 150 t/day: ~49% — dedicated rental begins to be operationally rational if demand/offtake is stable.
- Ownership: only evaluate after sustained >60–70% useful utilization, measured downtime/maintenance, secure multi-year feed/offtake and a payback case acceptable to founder/investors. This is a decision rule, not an automatic purchase trigger.

## Major implementation block
**Objective:** replace ROM with comparable real quotes while avoiding premature CAPEX.  
**Expected result:** ≥3 comparable offers for loader/excavator and ≥3 options for crusher/screen duty (host/shared/campaign/monthly), plus in-kind options.  
**Recommended tools:** standardized RFQ PDF/email, quote register, equipment comparison sheet, phone/WhatsApp follow-up.  
**Concrete steps:** issue non-binding RFQ after founder approval -> normalize included hours/fuel/operator/mobilization/wear -> score -> negotiate pilot duty -> document availability.  
**Validation checklist:** same duty basis; all exclusions; taxes; downtime; transport; fuel; operator; wear; availability.  
**Approved:** quote is complete enough to produce $/operating-hour and $/incoming-tonne scenarios.  
**Not approved:** headline monthly rate without mobilization/wear/fuel/downtime terms.  
**Risks:** comparing bare vs wet hire; hidden jaw/screen wear; low-bed cost; importing equipment for low utilization; no replacement on failure.  
**Delegation:** procurement/equipment specialist + site HSE/operations lead; counsel for contracts.  
**Exact next action:** obtain three non-binding local quotes for loader/excavator and ask the existing/public debris-system operator whether crusher/screen access can be scheduled by campaign before approaching imported rental.
