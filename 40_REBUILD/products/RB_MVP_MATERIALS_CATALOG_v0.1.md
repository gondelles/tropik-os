## Decision rule

Products are selected for reconstruction usefulness, debris-volume absorption, low process complexity, modest incremental CAPEX, manageable environmental burden, understandable buyer/use and achievable QA. Technological novelty alone is not a reason to launch a product.

All prices, yields and market-demand statements below are **WORKING HYPOTHESES** until local buyer quotes, specifications and tests exist.

## Candidate detail

### 1. Recycled Concrete Road Base 0–40
- **Plain-language subtitle:** 0–40 mm recycled concrete/mineral base for approved non-structural road-base, sub-base and temporary access-road applications.
- **Source waste:** source-separated or safely sorted concrete-rich debris.
- **Process:** pre-sort -> remove rebar/contamination -> crush -> magnet -> screen -> sample/test -> released stockpile.
- **Equipment:** excavator/loader, jaw crusher, magnet, screen, scale, dust suppression.
- **Testing:** gradation; contamination; moisture-density/compaction; CBR or local bearing requirement; abrasion/durability as required; chemistry/leachate where relevant.
- **Exact intended use:** approved road base/sub-base and temporary access roads.
- **Prohibited use:** structural concrete, untested drainage-sensitive use, any application outside approved spec.
- **Likely buyer:** public-works contractor, road contractor, reconstruction project, aggregate distributor.
- **Demand logic:** road/access rehabilitation consumes material quickly and can absorb meaningful tonnage.
- **Replacement product:** virgin crushed aggregate/base.
- **WORKING price reference:** ex-yard $6–14/t; **local quote TBD**.
- **Consumption speed:** high.
- **Potential debris absorption:** high; 20–55% of received tonnes depending feed/product qualification.
- **Margin potential:** medium; logistics can dominate.
- **Environmental concerns:** dust, runoff, noise, possible high-pH/tufa behavior in some recycled concrete applications.
- **Technical risk:** medium.
- **Commercial risk:** medium.
- **Status:** GREEN for validation, not yet commercial.

### 2. Recycled Mineral Fill
- **Subtitle:** screened recycled mineral material for approved non-structural fill, backfill, grading and terracing.
- **Source:** mixed clean concrete/masonry/mineral fraction after contamination removal.
- **Process:** sort -> optional crush -> screen -> QA.
- **Equipment:** loader/excavator; crusher/screen only as required.
- **Testing:** gradation; plasticity/fines where relevant; compaction/moisture-density; contamination; local fill specification.
- **Use:** approved non-structural fill/backfill/site grading.
- **Prohibited:** structural bearing claims without design; coastal dumping; drainage use without suitability check.
- **Buyer:** earthworks contractor, public reconstruction, civil contractor.
- **Replacement:** quarry fill/borrow material.
- **WORKING price:** $3–9/t ex-yard; local quote TBD.
- **Consumption:** very high.
- **Volume absorption:** very high.
- **Margin:** low–medium; attractive mainly when haul distances are favorable or disposal/service economics support it.
- **Environmental concerns:** fines/runoff/dust.
- **Status:** GREEN for validation.

### 3. Recovered Ferrous Steel Scrap
- **Subtitle:** sorted ferrous steel/rebar recovered from approved debris for sale to an authorized recycler/industrial buyer.
- **Source:** reinforced concrete/rebar/steel items.
- **Process:** mechanical separation -> magnet/manual confirmation -> safe cutting/bundling if needed -> weigh -> dispatch.
- **Equipment:** excavator, magnet, bins; cutting equipment only under controlled hot-work procedure if required.
- **Testing:** buyer contamination/grade requirements; radiation or other checks only if buyer/regulation requires.
- **Use:** recycling feedstock.
- **Prohibited:** direct structural reuse as rebar without specific engineering/legal approval.
- **Buyer:** scrap recycler/steel processor.
- **Replacement:** conventional scrap feedstock.
- **WORKING price:** ferrous $160–300/t ex-yard-equivalent; local buyer quote TBD.
- **Consumption:** fast if recycler relationship exists.
- **Volume absorption:** low–medium, value density high.
- **Margin:** potentially high relative to weight.
- **Environmental concerns:** safe storage, oils/contamination, cutting emissions/fire.
- **Status:** GREEN for validation.

### 4. Recycled Masonry Aggregate
- **Subtitle:** crushed/screened masonry-rich mineral aggregate for approved non-structural base, fill or landscaping/earthworks applications.
- **Source:** brick/block/mortar/masonry-rich debris.
- **Process:** sort -> crush -> screen -> QA.
- **Testing:** gradation, absorption, durability, contamination, compaction/use-specific properties.
- **Use:** only applications approved by engineer/buyer after testing.
- **Prohibited:** structural concrete aggregate by default; unverified drainage layer.
- **Buyer:** civil contractor, public works, aggregate distributor.
- **Replacement:** lower-grade virgin aggregate/fill.
- **WORKING price:** $5–12/t.
- **Consumption:** medium-high.
- **Volume absorption:** medium-high.
- **Margin:** medium if source separation is good.
- **Environmental concerns:** high fines/absorption; dust.
- **Status:** GREEN/YELLOW pending variability testing.

### 5. Reusable Construction Components
- **Subtitle:** individually inspected salvage items for a specifically approved second use.
- **Source:** doors, fixtures, pavers, metal frames, sanitary fixtures, selected timber/architectural items where clean and safe.
- **Process:** recover -> tag/photo -> clean -> condition assessment -> store -> assign approved use.
- **Testing:** item/use specific; structural components require qualified engineering review.
- **Use:** case-by-case non-structural reuse by default.
- **Prohibited:** blanket structural claims; sale of contaminated/damaged life-safety items.
- **Buyer/user:** reconstruction project, property owner, NGO/community program, architectural reuse market.
- **Replacement:** new component.
- **WORKING price:** item-based, not tonne-based; no public range until inventory exists.
- **Consumption:** variable.
- **Volume absorption:** low.
- **Margin:** potentially high per item, labor/space intensive.
- **Environmental concerns:** cleaning, storage, contamination.
- **Status:** YELLOW selective MVP lane.

### 6. Drainage Aggregate 20–40
- Intended for drainage layers only after particle durability, fines, chemistry and local specification are validated. Recycled concrete can be suitable in some granular applications but drainage systems can be sensitive to precipitate/fines behavior. **YELLOW.**

### 7. Pipe Bedding / Utility Aggregate
- Potentially useful in utility reconstruction but requires strict particle-size, cleanliness and project-specific bedding specification. **YELLOW.**

### 8. Temporary Access-Road Aggregate
- Strong immediate reconstruction use; operationally can be a specification/use variant of Road Base rather than a separate SKU. **GREEN as use case; avoid catalog duplication.**

### 9. Recycled Pavers / Blocks
- Requires batching, cement/binder, molds/press, curing, water, QA and additional environmental footprint. Do not add until aggregate streams and demand are proven. **YELLOW/RED.**

### 10. RCA for New Structural Concrete
- Technically possible in some jurisdictions, but Re:Build must not enter this use without local standards, source control, concrete mix design and full materials/structural validation. **RED for MVP.**

### 11. Clean Wood/Fiber Stream
- Only if demonstrably clean and a buyer/use exists. Painted/treated/contaminated wood must not enter a generic fiber stream. **YELLOW/RED.**

### 12. Re:Grow BioComposite
- Mycelium/bio-bound concepts remain **R&D, non-structural**. No MVP economics, performance or environmental claim. **RED for launch.**

## Naming architecture

Visible product name should stay short and credible:

`Re:Build + MATERIAL/FUNCTION + GRADE/SIZE (when meaningful)`

Back-office SKU:

`RB-[FUNCTION]-[GRADE]-[SOURCE/BATCH optional]`

Product lifecycle statuses:
`CONCEPT -> R&D -> PROTOTYPE -> FIELD TRIAL -> TESTED -> AVAILABLE -> HOLD -> RETIRED`

Never place unverified performance claims in the visible name.

# Environmental gate

A product cannot remain GREEN if testing or operation shows uncontrolled dust/runoff, hazardous carryover, excessive additional binder/energy burden, unacceptable fines/chemistry, or no responsible end-of-life/use route.

Prevention hierarchy: **avoid -> reduce -> reuse -> recover -> recycle -> responsibly dispose.**

# Major implementation block

**Objective:** reduce the catalog to products that can be tested, sold/redeployed and understood immediately.  
**Expected result:** 5 MVP product data sheets with defined batch specification and buyer trial.  
**Recommended tools:** lab partner, simple product-spec template, buyer interview sheet, weigh/batch records, WooCommerce catalog later.  
**Concrete steps:** obtain representative samples; agree test methods; run tests; obtain 3 buyer/contractor interviews per product; compare delivered cost vs substitute; execute one controlled field trial.  
**Validation checklist:** source known; prohibited uses explicit; test protocol agreed; buyer/use identified; price quote obtained; environmental gate passed; batch traceable.  
**Approved criterion:** at least one mineral product and ferrous scrap have a credible buyer/use and repeatable QA route.  
**Not approved:** product requires vague “sustainable material” positioning, structural claims without evidence, or extra manufacturing CAPEX before demand proof.  
**Risks/common errors:** too many SKUs; mixing concrete and masonry without tracking; pricing above virgin aggregate without delivered-cost logic; ignoring fines/absorption/chemistry; treating salvage as automatically safe.  
**Delegation:** materials/civil engineer + accredited/credible local lab; buyer technical staff; environmental/HSE specialist.  
**Exact next action:** ask three reconstruction/public-works buyers for the exact grading/CBR/compaction/contamination requirements they would accept for a 20–50 t pilot Road Base or Mineral Fill batch.
