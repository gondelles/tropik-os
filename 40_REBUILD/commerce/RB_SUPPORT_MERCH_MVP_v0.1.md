# RB_SUPPORT_MERCH_MVP_v0.1

**Status:** PRIVATE_WORKING  
**Venture:** Tropik Re:Build  
**Updated:** 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Re:Build Support Merch is a **separate commerce layer**, not recovered-material output and not automatically a charitable donation mechanism.

Objectives:
- raise unrestricted project-support cash where legally/accountingly appropriate;
- create awareness and community identity;
- give individuals a low-friction way to participate;
- document the mission without exploiting disaster victims.

**PROHIBITED CLAIM:** do not state that a fixed percentage or amount is donated to victims, communities or reconstruction unless the legal/accounting mechanism and beneficiary allocation are formally approved and auditable.

## Operating strategy

### USA
**STRATEGIC DECISION:** POD first through Printify/WooCommerce. No inventory commitment before demand evidence.

### LATAM / Venezuela
**STRATEGIC DECISION:** preorder or micro-batch only after demand. Do not stock large quantities. Validate local print quality, payment, delivery and unit economics first.

## Working price architecture

All values are **WORKING HYPOTHESES** and must be refreshed before launch.

- USA T-shirt retail target: **$28–34**.
- USA cap retail target: **$30–38**.
- Target gross margin before payment fees/returns/marketing: **~35–50%** where provider/shipping mix allows.
- LATAM/Venezuela: price from local landed unit cost with minimum **35% gross margin target**, but affordability and fulfillment reliability outrank copying USA pricing.

POD reference products include Bella+Canvas 3001 or comparable quality tee; caps should use simple embroidery-ready dad/baseball/trucker styles. Use one print area / simple embroidery for first launch.

## GREEN
Prototype only the top **2 tees + 1 cap** first. Order founder samples, verify print/embroidery quality, landed cost, fulfillment time and product page language.

## YELLOW
Prepare remaining designs and LATAM supplier quotes.

## RED
- large inventory buy;
- paid ads before organic/conversion proof;
- statements that merch revenue equals donations;
- victim/poverty imagery;
- disaster casualty imagery;
- political propaganda;
- copying NGO/government insignia;
- launching eight SKUs simultaneously.

# Major implementation block

**Objective:** validate whether supporters will buy mission-aligned merch without tying up cash.  
**Expected result:** 3 sampled products, true landed margins and first organic sales.  
**Recommended tools:** existing WooCommerce, Printify USA, local printer quotes, simple margin sheet.  
**Concrete steps:** prepare 2 tee concepts + 1 cap; upload POD samples; order samples; photograph; calculate landed margin; publish only after founder approval and legal wording review.  
**Validation checklist:** product quality; size/color reliability; delivery; return policy; ≥35% gross-margin target; no donation ambiguity; mission connection clear.  
**Approved criterion:** at least 10 organic/preorder sales with acceptable fulfillment and margin before adding SKUs or inventory.  
**Not approved:** margin <30%, quality inconsistent, misleading support language, or stock required to test demand.  
**Risks/common errors:** too many SKUs, expensive multi-location printing, confusing merch with donations, shipping eroding margin, disaster-exploitation creative.  
**Delegation:** graphic designer after concept approval; accountant/legal review for support/fundraising language; local printer for LATAM samples.  
**Exact next action:** prototype `Re:Build La Guaira Tee`, `Build Back Useful Tee`, and `Re:Build Field Cap`; do not publish until samples and margins are approved.
