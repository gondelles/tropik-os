## 90-day funding envelope
Steady C scenario: 3 × $78.87k OPEX = ~$236.6k plus portable/site setup. A realistic gross 90-day resource envelope is therefore roughly **$270k–$325k**, depending on what the host provides. With 35% documented in-kind support, indicative cash need becomes roughly **$176k–$211k**. This is not funding secured.

The commissioning ramp 25 -> 50 -> 75 t/day should cost less than a full three months at 65 t/day if crusher/screen is hired by campaign; obtain quotes before fixing the grant ask.

## Commercial break-even

### Can material sales alone finance the operation?
**NO under current RB-03B pilot assumptions.** P/C/O material+ferrous revenue per incoming tonne ($5.93/$11.37/$18.18) is far below modeled cash OPEX per incoming tonne ($85.97/$50.56/$42.19).

In the C case, even 100% saleable mineral at $9.50/t plus 3.6% saleable ferrous at $170/t produces only ~$15.62/incoming t, still below ~$50.56/t OPEX. Therefore volume/yield improvement alone cannot close the pilot model at these prices.

### Required blended model
Material sales + ferrous + authorized service/gate fee + equipment/logistics in-kind + public/recovery support. Merch is supplemental and must not be used to hide the operating gap.

Indicative service fee before institutional support: P ~$80/t; C ~$39/t; O ~$24/t. If planning support of $10k/$35k/$50k per month and merch contribution of 35% on $2k/$5k/$10k gross were actually secured, remaining service/funding gap is approximately P ~$59/t; C ~$15.6/t; O ~$10.3/t.

## Fatal assumptions
1. no site/operating authority;
2. no debris routing/feedstock control;
3. no output ownership/use right;
4. no safe hazardous/residual pathway;
5. no buyer/project specification or redeployment use;
6. Re:Build must pay full inbound + outbound haul at long distance;
7. dedicated crusher/screen must be paid monthly at 25–75 t/day;
8. no service/public/in-kind funding while mineral prices remain low.

## Major implementation block
**Objective:** convert ROM to quote-backed economics and expose the funding gap.  
**Expected result:** cost/incoming t and cost/saleable t anchored to real quotes, route km and buyer prices.  
**Recommended tools:** quote register, weigh tickets, equipment hour logs, route km, simple spreadsheet model.  
**Concrete steps:** quote site/equipment/lab/disposal/trucking -> interview buyers -> run 30-load characterization -> update yields -> calculate delivered contribution -> negotiate service/in-kind stack.  
**Validation checklist:** every major cost has source/date/unit; cash vs in-kind separated; haul responsibility explicit; outputs tied to buyer spec.  
**Approved:** C case has a documented payer/funder path for the residual gap with ≥90-day working capital.  
**Not approved:** materials revenue is presented as self-financing before measured economics support it.  
**Risks:** hidden transport, wear parts, double handling, long receivables, overvalued in-kind, optimistic scrap, unpriced residual.  
**Delegation:** accountant/tax advisor; procurement specialist; materials engineer; grant/funding specialist.  
**Exact next action:** collect the first 10 comparable quotes/data points that replace the largest C-case uncertainty: crusher/screen campaign access, loader/excavator, inbound/outbound trucking, residual pathway, lab pack and Road Base/Fill buyer price.
