## Commercial thesis

**WORKING HYPOTHESIS:** first mineral offtake should be validated with an active road/earthworks/public-works contractor able to approve a bounded non-structural trial quickly. Ferrous scrap should run as a separate recycler offtake lane because its buyer rules and value density are different.

No LOI, contract, buyer commitment, price or volume is currently assumed to exist.

## Initial payer/user recommendation

**STRATEGIC DECISION — PROPOSED:** validate a **contractor-led trial** first rather than waiting for municipal procurement. Ideal counterpart: contractor already performing road, earthworks, temporary access or site-grading works within a short haul radius of the selected hub. Technical decision maker = project/civil/pavement engineer; economic decision maker = project manager/procurement/commercial lead.

## Buyer interview questionnaire

### Organization / project
1. Organization, project, location, contact.
2. What materials do you buy/use today: base, subbase, fill, crushed stone, sand, scrap, reuse?
3. Which exact application consumes the most tonnes in the next 90–180 days?

### Current material and specification
4. Current material name/grade and supplier.
5. Exact project specification / COVENIN / owner specification / drawing reference.
6. Required sieve envelope/top size.
7. Required CBR, Proctor/field density, plasticity, abrasion/durability, absorption and chemistry limits.
8. Maximum permitted contamination and prohibited constituents.
9. Is recycled C&D mineral material explicitly allowed, conditionally allowed or prohibited?
10. Who can approve a material deviation or trial?

### Volume and economics
11. Monthly tonnes or m³.
12. Typical order/load size.
13. Current price and unit; ex-yard vs delivered.
14. Average haul distance and who pays freight.
15. Current quality/supply problems.
16. Minimum order and preferred delivery window.
17. Payment terms, credit days, retention/taxes if relevant.

### Trial willingness
18. Would you evaluate a traceable recycled trial batch if it meets your specification?
19. Minimum useful trial quantity: 20 / 50 / 100 / 250+ t?
20. Tests required before delivery.
21. Field test/placement requirements.
22. Expected price discount/premium vs virgin substitute.
23. Who signs technical acceptance? Who approves purchase?
24. Procurement/vendor-registration steps and lead time.
25. What result would make you buy/redeploy a second batch?

## Trial material offer — data fields
- Re:Build product/SKU and batch ID.
- Trial-only status; no blanket compliance claim.
- Source and process summary.
- Proposed application and explicit prohibited applications.
- Available tonnes.
- Nominal grading + attached lab results.
- Buyer/project specification being tested.
- Pickup/delivery options and haul assumptions.
- Trial price or zero-price/in-kind status only after founder approval.
- QA HOLD/PASS status.
- Placement/field test plan.
- Responsibility split: delivery, placement, compaction, field testing, rejection/return.
- Data permission and confidentiality.
- Non-binding wording until commercial/legal approval.

## Non-binding EOI / LOI minimum data requirements
A non-binding expression of interest should identify: organization; project; material; intended use; estimated trial tonnes; indicative monthly/90-day volume; required specification; target location; desired date; pickup/delivery; technical approver; commercial approver; prerequisites; testing required; indicative pricing basis if discussed; procurement path; payment terms; explicit non-binding clause; no exclusivity unless separately approved; validity period; signatures/contacts.

**PROHIBITED CLAIM:** an interview, quote request or EOI is not a committed offtake contract.

## Delivered vs ex-yard rule
Always quote both where possible. Low-value mineral products should not subsidize long haul invisibly. Record $/load, payload, one-way km, round-trip km and $/t freight separately from material price.

## Major implementation block
**Objective:** prove an actual use, spec, quantity and payer before scaling production.  
**Expected result:** ≥3 technical buyer interviews for Road Base/Fill, ≥3 recycler quotes for ferrous and one documented material trial path.  
**Recommended tools:** phone interview form, buyer spec upload, simple CRM/Buyer Register, quote comparison sheet.  
**Concrete steps:** identify active projects -> interview technical lead -> obtain written spec -> price/haul benchmark -> offer bounded test batch -> record trial outcome -> request non-binding EOI only after interest is real.  
**Validation checklist:** use, spec, tonnes, price basis, haul responsibility, approver, procurement, payment and trial gate known.  
**Approved:** buyer technical lead accepts the test plan and economic buyer identifies a procurement path.  
**Not approved:** generic enthusiasm without spec/volume/authority or only verbal price statements.  
**Risks:** selling before QA, ignoring freight, treating a user as payer, slow public procurement, long payment terms, trial turning into free open-ended supply.  
**Delegation:** founder/commercial lead; materials engineer for technical interview; counsel for LOI wording; accountant for taxes/payment terms.  
**Exact next action:** book three 20-minute interviews with active road/earthworks contractors and ask them to send the exact base/fill specification they currently buy against.
