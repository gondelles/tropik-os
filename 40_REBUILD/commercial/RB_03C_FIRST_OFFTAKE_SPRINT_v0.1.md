# 1. Objective

Find the fastest credible bounded technical use for the first Re:Build mineral batch and convert buyer interest into an executable `SPEC -> TEST -> TRIAL -> DISPOSITION -> REPEAT` path, while validating ferrous scrap separately.

## Expected result

- 3 buyer archetypes interviewed with named technical/economic approvers;
- exact current material/specification and delivered/ex-yard economics captured;
- one written technical trial path for Road Base 0–40 or Mineral Fill;
- one laboratory request tied to that buyer/project specification;
- 50–100 t traceable trial batch target where practical;
- 3 current ferrous recycler quotes/requirements and one 10–20 t trial path;
- no generic LOI substituted for technical acceptance.

## Recommended tools

Buyer Register; 20-minute interview form; WhatsApp/email follow-up; spec upload folder; quote/haul comparison sheet; materials engineer on technical calls; simple CRM/status board.

# 2. First buyer targets

## BUY-TARGET-01 — Active road / earthworks / public-works contractor

**Priority:** 1 / GREEN TO INTERVIEW.  
**Why:** likely to consume meaningful tonnes and already has a technical specification/engineer.  
**Best bounded uses:** temporary access road, subbase/base where explicitly approved, platform/grading fill.  
**Decision makers:** project/civil/pavement engineer; project manager/procurement/commercial lead.  
**Evidence target:** actual spec + current supplier/price + trial quantity + technical approver + procurement path.

## BUY-TARGET-02 — Private civil/developer/logistics project with fill/access-road demand

**Priority:** 2 / GREEN TO INTERVIEW.  
**Why:** potentially faster approval and lower procurement friction.  
**Best bounded uses:** non-structural fill, grading, temporary access/working platform under engineer approval.  
**Decision makers:** project engineer; owner/developer/project manager/procurement.  
**Evidence target:** site need within 90–180 days, fill/base spec, delivered economics, trial willingness.

## BUY-TARGET-03 — Aggregate/material distributor or technically credible material user

**Priority:** 3 / GREEN TO INTERVIEW.  
**Why:** can validate repeatability, market substitute, handling/storage and resale margin; may reveal buyer standards across multiple projects.  
**Best bounded use:** consistent tested mineral product, not ungraded mixed rubble.  
**Decision makers:** technical/quality lead where present; owner/commercial/procurement.  
**Evidence target:** accepted grading/contamination, monthly volume, price logic, collection/delivery, supplier onboarding.

## Separate lane — BUY-FE-01+ Ferrous recycler/processor

Do not mix ferrous economics/technical acceptance with mineral offtake.

# 3. Buyer interview execution plan

## Step 1 — Build target list

Create at least 3 organizations per mineral archetype = 9 target accounts, then select first 5 calls based on active project proximity to Santa and known material demand.

Do not rank by prestige; rank by **active tonnes + accessible technical approver + short trial path + short haul**.

## Step 2 — 20-minute technical-commercial interview

### Project / use
1. What active project/use will consume base/fill/aggregate in the next 90–180 days?
2. Exact location and approximate one-way haul from Santa?
3. Current material name/grade and exact application?
4. Tonnes or m³ needed per week/month and total remaining?

### Exact specification
5. Send the exact project/owner/COVENIN/contract specification or relevant drawing/BOQ section.
6. Required top size and sieve envelope?
7. Atterberg/plasticity requirements?
8. Proctor/field compaction requirement?
9. CBR/bearing requirement?
10. Abrasion/durability/absorption requirements?
11. Chemistry/leachate/chloride/sulphate requirements, if any?
12. Contamination/prohibited constituent limits?
13. Is recycled C&D mineral explicitly allowed, prohibited, or subject to material approval/deviation?
14. Who can approve a trial or deviation in writing?

### Current supplier/economics
15. Current supplier/source?
16. Current ex-yard price + unit?
17. Current delivered price + unit?
18. Typical load size/payload?
19. Who pays/arranges freight?
20. Current supplier pain: availability, quality, haul, price, timing?

### Trial path
21. Would the engineer evaluate a traceable recycled batch for a bounded non-structural use?
22. Minimum useful trial: 20 / 50 / 100 / 250+ t?
23. Tests required **before** delivery?
24. Field tests/placement supervision required?
25. Lift thickness/moisture/compaction procedure?
26. What constitutes PASS/FAIL after placement?
27. Rejection/removal responsibility?
28. What evidence would justify a second batch?

### Procurement
29. Technical approver name/role/contact?
30. Economic buyer name/role/contact?
31. Vendor registration/procurement steps and lead time?
32. Payment terms/credit days/retention/tax requirements?
33. Purchase order required before delivery?
34. Acceptable recycled-material pricing logic: parity / discount / performance-based / delivered-cost basis?

## Step 3 — Same-day evidence follow-up

Ask for four items only:
1. exact spec/document;
2. current quote/invoice/price basis if shareable;
3. trial approval/contact path;
4. proposed trial site/date/quantity.

Verbal enthusiasm without these remains `INTEREST ONLY`.

# 6. First product trial selection

## STRATEGIC DECISION RULE

Choose the first mineral product **after buyer interviews**, not because it scores highest in the desk catalog.

### Score each real trial path 0–2

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
|Exact spec received|none|partial|complete written|
|Recycled material pathway|prohibited/unknown|deviation possible|explicit trial allowed|
|Technical approver|unknown|identified|engaged/agrees plan|
|Bounded use risk|broad/structural|moderate|clearly non-structural/bounded|
|Processing complexity|high|medium|low|
|Test package/turnaround|unknown/slow|manageable|clear/fast|
|Trial tonnes|impractical|possible|50–100 t practical|
|Haul/delivered economics|weak/unknown|borderline|credible short-haul|
|Placement/field test|unclear|manageable|clear PASS/FAIL|
|Repeat demand|none/unknown|possible|active volume|

Maximum 20. First trial should normally score ≥15 with no fatal zero in specification, technical approval or bounded use.

### WORKING HYPOTHESIS — default if evidence ties

If Road Base and Mineral Fill have equally credible buyers, **start with Re:Build Mineral Fill** because it can often use a simpler processing/test route and a tightly bounded non-structural application. Switch to **Road Base 0–40** if the first credible buyer provides a clear project specification, recycled-material approval path and high-value repeat demand.

**This is not a final product selection until interviews are completed.**

# 7. Trial batch protocol

Flow:

`SOURCE LOADS -> BATCH -> PROCESS -> QA HOLD -> LAB -> PASS/FAIL -> TRANSPORT -> PLACEMENT -> FIELD TESTS -> BUYER/ENGINEER DISPOSITION -> REPEAT/CLOSE`

## Batch identity

`BATCH-[SKU]-YYYYMMDD-##`

Minimum batch record:
- Batch ID / SKU;
- source Load IDs;
- source strata;
- incoming mineral tonnes;
- process date/time;
- crusher/screen settings where used;
- magnet/separation steps;
- nominal size;
- batch tonnes;
- operator/equipment IDs;
- water/dust-control note;
- visible contamination;
- sample IDs;
- HOLD/PASS/FAIL/CONDITIONAL status;
- finished stockpile location;
- buyer/project specification reference.

## Target size

**TARGET:** 50–100 t where practical and requested by buyer. Do not create excess product without a confirmed trial/hold space.

## Physical segregation rule

Trial batch remains separately labeled and physically segregated while on HOLD. No dispatch until required QA disposition exists.

- CBR;
- abrasion/durability;
- absorption/specific gravity;
- contamination/composition review;
- chemistry/chlorides/sulphates/pH/leachate where source/use risk or specification requires.

## Minimum qualification package — Mineral Fill planning envelope

- gradation/fines;
- Atterberg if relevant/plastic fines present;
- Modified Proctor MDD/OMC or project compaction reference;
- contamination;
- CBR only if bearing function/project spec requires;
- chemistry/leachate/salts/organics where source/receptor/spec requires.

## Lab response required before sampling is finalized

- exact method/standard for each test;
- sample mass/number required;
- preparation requirements;
- accreditation/competence evidence for requested methods;
- price by test/package;
- turnaround;
- report format;
- chain-of-custody acceptance;
- retained sample policy;
- re-test rules.

**PROHIBITED CLAIM:** passing a benchmark test does not make the product generally COVENIN-approved or approved for all uses.

# 10. Buyer acceptance gate

### PASS — TECHNICAL TRIAL PATH
All of the following exist:
- exact bounded application;
- written project specification or written trial criteria;
- named technical approver;
- required lab tests/methods/limits;
- trial quantity/date/site;
- field-placement/test plan;
- rejection responsibility;
- procurement/economic buyer path;
- haul responsibility and delivered/ex-yard logic;
- written disposition after trial.

### NOT PASS
- generic sustainability interest;
- generic LOI without spec/approver/use;
- product delivered before lab/HOLD release;
- only a verbal price;
- “use it anywhere as fill” with no engineer/site acceptance;
- hidden free-supply expectation;
- broad compliance language.

---

# 11. Pricing rule

Record separately:

`material ex-yard $/t + outbound freight $/t + handling/testing-specific cost if applicable = delivered cost $/t`

Compare against buyer's actual substitute **delivered** price. Never hide freight inside a material price when evaluating low-value mineral economics.

No trial price, discount, free material or commercial commitment without founder approval.

# 14. Approved / Not approved criterion

**APPROVED FOR FIRST BATCH:** buyer technical lead accepts written test/placement plan and economic buyer identifies a real procurement/second-batch path; source/batch and site/output rights are also cleared.

**NOT APPROVED:** buyer interest exists but specification, approver, field acceptance or economics remain unknown.

---

# 15. Risks and common errors

- asking “would you buy recycled aggregate?” instead of asking for the actual spec;
- over-optimizing a lab package before knowing buyer requirements;
- treating distributor interest as technical acceptance;
- trial material becoming uncontrolled free supply;
- ignoring return/rejection liability;
- pricing against ex-yard virgin while Re:Build pays delivery;
- confusing user, payer, engineer and procurement decision maker;
- using COVENIN benchmark language as a blanket compliance claim.

---

- Lab: method/sample/turnaround evidence.

- Counsel/accountant: commercial terms, title, tax/payment/contract structure before binding offer.
