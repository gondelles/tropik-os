## Current strategic position

- **STRATEGIC DECISION:** no demolition activity.
- **STRATEGIC DECISION:** do not build a standalone greenfield plant for MVP.
- **STRATEGIC DECISION:** seek one dedicated 0.5–1.5 ha operating cell inside or adjacent to an authorized debris-management site.
- **WORKING HYPOTHESIS:** Santa Eduvigis / Urimare remains Candidate #1; Caraballeda / Los Corales #2; Catia La Mar / Av. El Ejército / Playa Grande #3.
- **TARGET:** base engineering footprint = 10,000 m²; minimum 5,000 m² under restricted throughput/stock conditions; preferred 10,000–15,000 m²; expansion 20,000–30,000 m².
- **STRATEGIC DECISION:** borrow/share/rent loader, excavator, crusher, screen and associated heavy equipment before any purchase decision.
- **TARGET:** first physical 90-day ramp remains 25 -> 50 -> 75 t/day, 3,600 t total at 24 operating days/month.
- **WORKING HYPOTHESIS:** formal planning scenarios = 25 / 65 / 150 t/day, with 300 t/day as later expansion case.
- **WORKING HYPOTHESIS:** crusher/screen nameplate capacity is not the expected MVP bottleneck; safe sorting, feed consistency, QA, stockpile discipline, equipment uptime and offtake are more likely constraints.
- **TARGET:** calibrated mass-balance closure ±3–5%.
- **PROHIBITED CLAIM:** no recovery percentage, structural performance, carbon reduction, product compliance, government authorization, funding award or output ownership may be stated publicly until independently validated.
