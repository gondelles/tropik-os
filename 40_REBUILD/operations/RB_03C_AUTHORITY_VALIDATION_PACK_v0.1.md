# 5. Site-validation meeting agenda — 75 minutes

1. **0–5 min — Purpose and boundaries:** validation only; no capital/site commitment.
2. **5–15 — Authority chain:** owner, operator, routing authority, output-rights signer.
3. **15–25 — Polygon + current operations:** map, traffic, tonnes, sources, current train.
4. **25–35 — Feedstock campaign:** permission for 30 loads, weighing/photos/separation/sampling.
5. **35–45 — HSE/environment:** host rules, suspect loads, drainage, dust, emergency.
6. **45–55 — Equipment/resources:** scale, loader/excavator, crusher/screen, water, trucks, security.
7. **55–65 — Output/product trial:** title, lab, 50–100 t batch, buyer/project trial, residual route.
8. **65–72 — Documents and owners:** walk through evidence request list.
9. **72–75 — Next decisions:** action owner/date; next meeting; fatal blockers.

**Meeting rule:** do not negotiate land lease, equipment purchase, permanent employment, wages, public fundraising or binding commercial prices in this meeting.

# 8. Risks and common errors

- mistaking a senior/public official for the actual authorized signatory;
- assuming total landfill acreage equals Re:Build usable area;
- treating debris access as output ownership;
- accepting verbal permission as land/site authority;
- ignoring existing contractor/recycler rights;
- assuming hazardous disposal exists because quarantine space exists;
- omitting scale calibration or final disposal receipts;
- publishing site/tonnage/equipment claims from the meeting before approval.
