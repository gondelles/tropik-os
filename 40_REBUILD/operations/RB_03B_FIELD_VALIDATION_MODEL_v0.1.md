## Purpose

## Assumption policy

Priority of evidence: verified public information -> Venezuela/La Guaira/LATAM benchmarks -> comparable C&D/disaster-debris operations -> manufacturer benchmarks -> engineering judgment -> conservative range.

## Fatal site gates
A site remains **YELLOW/RED**, never GREEN, until all are documented:
1. written site/operating authority;
2. debris-routing authority;
3. output ownership/use/sale rights;
4. surveyed polygon + usable operating cell;
5. all-weather 20–30 t truck access and safe turning;
6. HSE rules and accountable site HSE lead;
7. hazardous/suspect-material pathway;
8. drainage/runoff/flood/receptor/wind screening;
9. equipment access and maintenance responsibility;
10. weighing/traceability method;
11. residual authorized disposal pathway;
12. at least one laboratory/product-test route;
13. at least one buyer/reconstruction trial path;
14. emergency access, welfare and security.

## Site validation checklist — phone-first

### Identity and authority
- Site ID; date/time; interviewer; authority present.
- GPS point; polygon corners; photos/video sweep.
- Site owner/controller; operating contractor; written authority reference.
- Debris-routing authority; output-rights decision maker.

### Geometry and traffic
- Gross m²; usable m²; excluded m².
- Length/width pinch points; entry/exit width.
- Road surface; grade; bearing evidence; flood damage.
- Largest current truck; payload; observed turning path.
- Queue length; trucks/hour and trucks/day observed.
- Reversing points; pedestrian conflicts; emergency lane.

### Process and stock
- Current incoming tonnes/day and source mix if records exist.
- Existing stockpiles by type; estimated/known tonnes.
- Existing loader/excavator/breaker/crusher/screen/magnet/scale.
- Existing processing sequence and uptime.
- Available clean mineral, residual, quarantine and finished-product cells.

### Environment
- Drainage direction/outfall; storm drains; coastline/surface water proximity.
- Erosion/sediment evidence; standing water; flood marks.
- Prevailing wind observation and local wind records to obtain.
- Nearest homes/schools/clinics/businesses/roads/water bodies.
- Existing dust/noise controls; fuel/oil storage; spill evidence.

### Utilities/support
- Potable/process water source and capacity.
- Power/kVA; generator availability.
- Lighting/comms; welfare/toilets/wash; first aid.
- Security perimeter, access control and night security.

## Debris-load characterization methodology

### Minimum practical campaign
**TARGET:** 30 incoming 20-t-equivalent loads, ≈600 t total, across at least 5 materially different source/location/building-demolition categories where routing allows; target ≥6 loads/stratum. This is a practical pilot sample, not proof of regional representativeness.

**Minimum gate:** 20 loads / ≈400 t only if access is constrained; confidence is downgraded.  
**Detailed classification target:** mass-account every full load where safe. If impossible, conduct detailed weighed separation on at least 15 loads and visual/weight-ticket classification on all 30.

### Load record
- Load ID; source ID; source coordinates/zone; source type.
- Truck ID; gross/tare/net; weigh method/calibration.
- Arrival photos before tipping and spread-load photos.
- Moisture/weather note.
- Visual contamination score 0–4.
- Mass by concrete/mineral; masonry; ferrous; non-ferrous; wood/fiber; plastic/other recyclables; reusable components; fines; suspect/hazardous; residual.
- Processing time; equipment minutes; rejection/quarantine reason.
- Mass-balance closure %.

### Contamination score
0 = source-separated clean mineral; 1 = minor removable contamination; 2 = mixed but sortable; 3 = high contamination requiring slow segregation/quarantine; 4 = suspected hazardous/unacceptable — STOP/REJECT pending HSE.

### Representativeness rule
No composition figure may be called representative of La Guaira until source strata, sample distribution and variance support that statement. Report mean, median, range and per-source distribution before aggregating.

## Product sampling form
- Batch ID; product SKU; source load IDs.
- Process date; crusher/screen settings; nominal size.
- Batch tonnes; sampling method; sample mass; sampler.
- Visual contamination; moisture; retained sample location.
- Requested tests; lab; chain-of-custody seal/time.
- QA status HOLD / PASS / FAIL / CONDITIONAL.

## Laboratory request form
- Sample ID / Batch ID.
- Proposed use and prohibited uses.
- Buyer/project specification reference.
- Required method + result units.
- Requested minimum set: sieve/gradation; Atterberg where relevant; Modified Proctor; CBR; density/moisture; abrasion/durability; absorption/specific gravity; chlorides/sulphates/chemistry/leachate only where use/source requires.
- Turnaround; price; accreditation/competence evidence; sample quantity; reporting format.

## Machine inventory form
- Equipment ID; owner; make/model/year/serial.
- Capacity; feed size; bucket/deck/jaw details.
- Meter hours; condition; last service.
- Fuel/power; observed consumption if recorded.
- Operator included? maintenance included? wear parts responsibility?
- Availability windows; mobilization need; downtime/replacement terms.
- In-kind/rent/purchase status. Purchase remains RED.

## Decision-maker interview form
- Role; organization; authority scope; identity/status.
- What written approval is required?
- Who signs it?
- Evidence they require from Re:Build.
- Current debris route and operating constraints.
- Material ownership/output rights.
- HSE/environment rules.
- Equipment/site/logistics resources they could provide.
- Decision date/dependencies.
- Fatal/non-fatal blocker.

Unknown identities are recorded as **DECISION-MAKER IDENTITY TBD — FIELD VALIDATION REQUIRED.**

## HSE STOP-WORK conditions
Immediate STOP if any of the following is uncontrolled: suspect asbestos/friable fibrous material; chemical drums/unknown liquids; gas cylinders/explosive risk; medical/biological waste; human remains; active fire/smoke; unstable load/pile; major spill; failed machine guard/emergency stop; pedestrian/mobile-plant separation failure; dust suppression failure during visible high-dust crushing; severe weather/flooding/runoff escape; blocked emergency access; untrained operator; loss of communications for critical movements.

## LOAD-REJECTION / QUARANTINE criteria
Reject or quarantine loads with suspected asbestos, unknown powders/chemicals, pressurized cylinders, contaminated soils, medical waste, significant hydrocarbons, batteries/e-waste where no controlled pathway exists, unstable oversized pieces that cannot be safely tipped, or any material prohibited by host-site/environmental rules. Re:Build does not treat hazardous waste.

## Environmental screening
No uncontrolled discharge to coast, waterways or storm drains. Validate drainage catchment, sediment control, bunding, fuel/no-drain areas, dust boundary, noise receptors, flood exposure and prevailing wind before crushing. Coastal proximity increases—not reduces—the control burden.

## Major implementation block
**Objective:** replace fatal assumptions with measured field evidence.  
**Expected result:** one site and one material/buyer route capable of entering a controlled pilot gate.  
**Recommended tools:** phone camera/GPS, calibrated scale or weighbridge, Sheets/simple forms, tape/laser measure, site plan, PPE, sampling kit.  
**Concrete steps:** authority interview -> polygon/traffic/environment survey -> 30-load characterization -> product batches -> lab tests -> buyer trial -> quote capture.  
**Validation checklist:** every fatal gate has an owner, document and date; all tonnes traceable; all samples chain-of-custody.  
**Approved:** site + feedstock + product + buyer + HSE/environment + equipment/logistics route are documented.  
**Not approved:** any fatal condition remains unknown/uncontrollable.  
**Risks:** confusing public reports with authorization; biased sampling; uncalibrated weights; mixing batches; unsafe hand sorting; unrecorded in-kind resources.  
**Delegation:** Venezuelan counsel, civil/site engineer, materials/pavement engineer, environmental/HSE specialist, laboratory, procurement specialist.
