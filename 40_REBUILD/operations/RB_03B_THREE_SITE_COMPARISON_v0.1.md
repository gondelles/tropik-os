## Five architecture test

| Architecture | Trucks / handling | CAPEX/OPEX | Complexity | Resilience | Pilot verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1. Santa Eduvigis only|Lowest double handling; central haul|Lowest duplicated cost|Low|Medium|**GREEN/YELLOW — preferred pending fatal gates**|
|2. Caraballeda intake + Santa processing|Adds transfer haul and second handling|Higher|Medium|Medium-high|**YELLOW — only if source-side benefit exceeds transfer cost**|
|3. Santa central/east + Catia west|Reduces some long cross-state haul; duplicates site functions|High|High|High|**YELLOW/RED for 90-day pilot**|
|4. Catia primary large hub|Simple single-hub if authority/equipment/logistics superior|Low-medium|Low|Medium|**YELLOW — contingency/alternative**|
|5. Three-node network|Highest movements and duplication|Highest|Highest|Highest|**RED for initial pilot**|

## Strategic recommendation

**STRATEGIC DECISION — RB-03B PROPOSED:** validate **Architecture 1: Santa Eduvigis as the only staffed 90-day hub** first. Keep **Architecture 4: Catia La Mar as the primary fallback** if Santa fails a fatal gate. Treat Caraballeda as a source-side characterization/temporary transfer option, not as a permanent second site, until measured haul savings, feedstock control or authority justify duplication.

No physical site is GREEN today. Written authority, output rights, hazardous pathway, surveyed polygon, traffic/bearing, drainage/runoff, receptors/wind, utilities, equipment access, security, laboratory route and buyer trial remain required.

## Approval gate

**APPROVED for pilot engineering only if:** one site passes every fatal authority/HSE/environment/logistics gate and can support the planned stock dwell without blocking emergency/truck circulation.  
**NOT APPROVED:** site area is merely assumed; critical runoff/receptors unknown; output rights missing; hazardous route missing; or the network requires routine double handling without an economic offset.
